# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Transport owners, driver-owners and traders across Bangladesh (testimonials: আড়ৎ ব্যবসায়ী, ড্রাইভার, ব্যবসায়ী). Mostly on a phone, Bangla first, often outdoors between trips. They are looking for a used pickup, truck, bus, tipper or 3-wheeler at a clear price, close to home.

## Product Purpose
Gari Mela (Rangs Motors Ltd.) sells pre-owned commercial vehicles from 15 brands, cash or installment, out of depots in 30 districts. The site has to make one belief stick: "the vehicle I need is already sitting at a depot near me, at a clear price." Success = a call to 16758, or opening a real vehicle.

## Brand Personality
Warm, local, crafted. A showroom you'd trust, run by people you could call. Three palette themes (seasons of Bengal: Hemanta / harvest, Godhuli / twilight, Borsha / monsoon), each in light and dark. Emotional goals: recognition and pride in the hero, "it's near me" at the depot map, reassurance in the trust and voices sections, resolve at the close.

## Anti-references
- Beige "luxury" minimal: cream page backgrounds, thin serif type, everything muted.
- (From the original brief) Glass on everything; gradient decoration with no job; fabricated numbers.

## Design Principles
1. **Real over decorative.** Every number is sourced from the data snapshot or quoted from the client; the imagery is the client's own vehicles.
2. **Local craft as the human touch.** Bengali seasons, alpona line-art, handwritten notes and greetings carry warmth; the palette carries the theme.
3. **Glass where you touch, gradient where there's light.** Translucency marks interactive and floating layers; gradients model light (sky, horizon, sheen), not ornament.
4. **One action, always in reach.** Call 16758 is the primary action on every screen; secondary is opening a vehicle.
5. **Bangla first, phone first.** Big tap targets, Bangla digits, layouts designed at 375px before 1440px.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: body text 4.5:1, large text and UI 3:1 in all six theme/mode combinations. Keyboard paths and visible focus rings, 44px+ targets, `prefers-reduced-motion`, `prefers-reduced-transparency` and `prefers-contrast` respected. Meaning never by colour alone.
